## Academic evidence hierarchy

1. **Official awarding-body specifications and teacher-confirmed route** establish what a learner is expected to know and do.
2. **Teacher-approved DBSJP resource evidence** informs school-specific progression, explanation, misconceptions and modelled responses.
3. **Verified subject-specific public lesson pages** supply convenient direct student learning materials, but their content must not be assumed correct or identical to DBSJP without review.
4. **General subject catalogues** are supplementary navigation only; do not treat them as exact exam-board course mapping.
5. **AI** receives only brief, original course-level qualification scopes and URLs labelled metadata; it is not fed scraped third-party text or claimed to have browsed resources.
